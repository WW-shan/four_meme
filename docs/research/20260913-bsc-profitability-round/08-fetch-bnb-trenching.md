# BNB Trenching Szn is Back: Win a share of $200K on Flap and Four.meme

2026.8.21  •  1 min read

![Blog post image.](https://storage.ghost.io/c/f4/1b/f41b2cc7-c557-4fb7-82f5-ff223a27dcc6/content/images/2026/08/IMG_0314.png)

**TL;DR**

* BNB Trenching Szn is back and bringing two trading campaigns with 200,000 USDT in total rewards.
* Flap will run a bStocks meme ecosystem campaign focused on trading activity and daily rewards.
* Four.meme will run a 10-day PnL competition with 10,000 USDT distributed daily to the top 150 traders.

## **The BNB Trenches Are Open**

BNB Trenching Szn is back, bringing more ways for the community to trade, compete, and earn across BNB Chain, with 200,000 USDT in rewards up for grabs.

[Flap](https://flap.sh/board) and [Four.meme](https://four.meme/) will each run a different format, giving traders two ways to compete: one centered trading activity, and another around daily PnL performance. Here’s how to take part.

## **Two Ways Into the Trenches**

**Flap: Run Up the Volume**

BNB Chain and Flap are preparing a 100,000 USDT campaign for all Flap-tokens on BNB Chain. Daily airdrops will reward active traders as they trade over a certain trading volume.

* Competition Period: August 19 to August 28, 2026
* Reward Structure: Random daily airdrops based on trading volume >$500
* Total Prize Pool: 100,000 USDT

Find out more about the campaign [here](https://x.com/flapdotsh/status/2090114124093243439).

**Four.meme: Climb the PnL Leaderboard**

BNB Chain and Four.meme are bringing 100,000 USDT to a 10-day trading competition focused on PnL.

Each day, 10,000 USDT will be distributed to the top 150 traders ranked by daily PnL, giving traders a fresh leaderboard to compete on every day.

* Competition Period: August 24 to September 2, 2026
* Reward Structure: 10,000 USDT distributed daily to the top 150 traders by daily PnL
* Total Prize Pool: 100,000 USDT

Find out more about the campaign [here](https://x.com/fourdotmemezh/status/2091730284895232449?s=46&t=VPovUBtVmvRw4wun0RR8GA).

## **Double Down on this Szn**

BNB Trenching Szn gives traders two different ways to get involved.

Trade the bStocks meme ecosystem through Flap and compete for daily rewards based on activity, or put your trading performance to the test in Four.meme’s daily PnL rankings.
