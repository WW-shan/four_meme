How these rankings work

Activity uses distinct observed X authors. Rising compares distinct authors in the last 15 minutes with the preceding 15 minutes, with +1 smoothing. Acceleration stays unavailable until 30 minutes of continuous successful collection are recorded. The score is an unvalidated discovery heuristic.

Reposts, quotes and replies are separate post types. Engagement counts reported on original posts are cumulative snapshots, not additional observed posts. KOL means a configured account, not a verified promotion. GMGN platform visits are not X mentions.

Contract strings are matched exactly against observed chain-qualified candidates. A match is not proof of endorsement, an official token, liquidity or tradeability. Only configured sources are covered; this is not a whole-internet ranking.
